Personal

Three logo directions for an AI content studio that reads a creator's Instagram, ranks a daily For You feed, and drafts reels, carousels and captions. Each mark encodes what the product actually does.

Recommended 01 The Spark
personal

Why it fits

A single four-point spark stands in for the two jobs the product does at once — personalize and generate. It rides as a counter-accent to a quiet lowercase wordmark, so the name stays fully legible while the mark carries the meaning. The spark alone is the app icon and survives a 16px favicon without losing its read.
